# Hardware

Printable parts that hold the XIAO ESP32S3 Sense under a Meta Quest 3S, pointed at the mouth. Revision R14.

## Parts

| File | Part |
|---|---|
| [`CameraCaseWithAdapter.stl`](stl/CameraCaseWithAdapter.stl) | Case for the XIAO, including the joint to the mount |
| [`CameraCover.stl`](stl/CameraCover.stl) | Lid for the case |
| [`CentralMount.stl`](stl/CentralMount.stl) | Mount that attaches to the centre of the headset |

- Binary STL, units in millimetres.
- Designed for PETG on a Bambu Lab A1 with a 0.4 mm nozzle.
- Only the printable meshes are included. The CAD sources are not in this repository, and no Quest 3S or XIAO geometry is included.

## Other parts

| Part | Specification | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| Socket head cap screw | M3 × 20, coarse thread, standard head, fully threaded | 6 |
| Nylon-insert lock nut | M3, DIN 985, 5.5 mm across flats, 4 mm high or less | 6 |
| Silicone sheet | 0.5 mm thick, cut into washers and pads | 1 |
| Hex key | 2.5 mm | 1 |

You also need a USB-C cable and a 5 V supply, an external Wi-Fi antenna with a U.FL connector, and thin cable ties and a safety cord to hold the cables and keep the camera from falling.
